#### My memories of how I became interested in programming
From the beginning of my life until a few years ago, without any worries or turmoil, I would turn night into day and day into night.
I remember that I saw a post on a website, and the text of the post was converted to audio by a software.
This same factor aroused my curiosity. I really wanted to know about the process of converting text to audio.
I also wanted to convert a text to an audio file.
This incident made me go on the internet and look for a good text-to-speech converter. I saw interesting things.
I would download everything I found on the internet and install it on my laptop. I remember that I found a text-to-speech converter and installed it on my laptop, and it caused my laptop to break down.
I would always get very sad when my laptop broke down, and I would repeat out loud that if my laptop returns to its previous state, I won't go after anything and won't search anymore.
As soon as my laptop was fixed, my searches would start, and this cycle continued for several days.
On the internet, on a website, I read about the Python programming language. Until night fell, I was only researching and studying.
I was very afraid of installing programs, especially since my laptop would break down and I always had to beg my laptop to get fixed.
A bitter thought passed through my mind, I always thought that if I install Python on my laptop, it will break down. I couldn't sleep well that night. I thought to myself that I wrote a text-to-speech converter and I'm working with it.
For a few days, I only studied about programming languages, I read their codes, but I could only understand Python codes better.
At that time, my laptop's Windows was 7, and my laptop was old.
I remember that after doing a lot of research on Python, I could no longer resist not installing Python, and I downloaded Python.
When I was going to install it on my Windows, my laptop's Windows was 7 and the version of Python I had downloaded was much higher than the version supported by my laptop's Windows.
I looked for lower versions and installed that version, and I was able to progress through a good educational course.

ویژگی که دارم این هست که برنامه‌نویسی رو با پروژه نوشتن سعی می‌کنم بیشتر یادش بگیرم.
یه چیز صادقانه هم می‌خوام بگم، ممکنه وقتی متن روی پروفایل من رو می‌خونین زیاد دوستش نداشته باشید.

تلاش می‌کنم بتونم تا حدود خوبی یادش بگیرم، برای خودم توی گیتهاب رزومه درست کنم.
وقتی که حرفه‌ای شدم دنبال کار بگردم و پروژه‌های مختلف رو انجام بدم.
من به هک و امنیت هم خیلی علاقه دارم، می‌خوام علاوه بر برنامه‌نویسی هک و امنیت رو هم بلد باشم.
دوست دارم پروژه‌ای که در آینده قرار هست توسط من ساخته بشه، پروژه‌ی امنی باشه، دوست دارم حتی باگ‌های امنیتی یک پروژه رو هم خودم اصلاح کنم.
